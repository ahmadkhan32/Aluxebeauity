Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior beauty experience to take these courses?
No! Our foundational courses (Nail Tech, Brow Lamination, Classic Lashes) are designed specifically for beginners with no prior experience. We guide you step-by-step from foundational health and safety to advanced techniques.
Are the qualifications fully accredited and insurable?
Yes! All our certificates are accredited by ABT (Associated Beauty Therapists) or CPD. This allows you to secure professional public liability and treatment insurance with leading UK insurance providers immediately upon completion.
What is included in the student starter kit?
Each course includes a complete commercial kit containing professional-grade tools, adhesives, solutions, and disposables sufficient to perform treatments on your first 20–30 clients, meaning your kit pays for itself.
Can I get a job at one of your 26 salon branches?
Yes. Graduates of our Academy receive fast-track interview status and practical trade test priority for openings across our 26 UK branch concessions.
Do you offer split payments or instalment plans?
Yes. You can secure your place with a £100 deposit and pay the remaining balance on the first day of class, or arrange a flexible 3-part monthly instalment plan. Contact us on WhatsApp for details.
